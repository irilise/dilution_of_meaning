Lo! the glory of the Danes of days gone by, the might of the princes of that people, has been celebrated. Of Scyld Scefing and his son-folk the spear-arms; how those nobles played their part well. The chieftain Scyld had come in time of need, alone and helpless; he lived to find comfort, he waxed in power and wisdom until all his neighbors far and wide gave him ear.
