## Purpose
This collection is designed to test:
- **Memorization**: Mix of famous passages (which models may have seen in training) and obscure ones
- **Semantic Drift**: How meaning degrades through iterative summarization
- **Attractor Dynamics**: Whether LLMs converge to particular outputs or patterns
