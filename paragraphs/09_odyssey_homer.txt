Sing, O goddess, of the rage of Achilles, son of Peleus, that brought countless agonies upon the Achaeans. Many a brave soul did it send hurrying down to Hades, and many a hero did it yield a prey to dogs and vultures, for so were the counsels of Jove fulfilled from the day on which the sons of Atreus, king of men, and great Achilles, first fell out with one another.
